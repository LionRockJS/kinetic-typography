Structure · 起承轉合

起 is a short strike — the start point. 承 is the long stretch that spreads out from it. 轉 is the change point, a second or two. 合 resolves. After a 轉 comes another 承.

Two levels: the overall arc runs through the whole video and stays simple; the animation arc is the finer beat the layers move to, with its own span. Both are reference lines only — like a grid on a canvas.

A fallback stack. Each character is drawn with the first font that actually has a glyph for it — so a Latin display face can carry the headline while a CJK face picks up 起承轉合.

Outlines are extracted with opentype.js and triangulated into three.js meshes — every glyph is an independent object, so effects can move them one by one.
